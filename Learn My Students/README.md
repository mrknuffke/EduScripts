# 🎓 Learn My Students

> **A simple, private, zero-install flashcard tool for teachers to master student names and faces.**

`Version 1.0 · 2026-08-06`

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Safety Guarantee (Safe to Share!)

**Sharing `learn-my-students.html` and `README.md` together shares ZERO student information:**

- **`learn-my-students.html` and `README.md` are clean template files.** They contain **only application code and instructions**, and contain **NO** student names, photos, student IDs, or roster data. You can safely email, AirDrop, or share these two files with any colleague.
- **All student data stays strictly on your computer.** When you import a roster, student names and photos are processed directly inside your web browser on your machine. Nothing is ever sent to a server, cloud service, or external website.

> ⚠️ **Data Responsibility Warning:**
> **NEVER share or upload your PowerSchool `.pdf` roster files or saved `.deck.json` progress files.** Those files contain real student names and photographs of minors and must remain strictly on your personal computer.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide (3 Easy Steps)

### Step 1: Open the Tool
Double-click `learn-my-students.html`. It opens instantly in your normal web browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox). You do not need to install any software or be connected to the internet.

*💡 **Tip: Add to your Dock or Desktop App Icon***
- **Safari (Mac):** Open `learn-my-students.html` ➔ Click **File** in the Mac top menu ➔ Click **Add to Dock**.
- **Chrome or Edge:** Open `learn-my-students.html` ➔ Click the **Install** icon in the address bar (or **Menu ➔ Save and Share ➔ Create Shortcut... ➔ Open as Window**).

---

### Step 2: Export your Roster from PowerSchool
1. Log into PowerSchool.
2. Select your class section and run the **Photos: 12/page** report.
3. Save the report as a `.pdf` file on your computer.
   *💡 **Tip:** Name your PDF file after your class section (e.g. `AP_Bio_Period_1.pdf` or `Chemistry_A2.pdf`). The tool will automatically use the filename as your class name!*

---

### Step 3: Import & Practice!
1. **Import:** Drag and drop your `.pdf` roster file (or multiple PDF / `.deck.json` files at once!) into the drop zone below on the home page (or click **Browse Files**).
2. **Confirm:** Check the extracted student photos and names on the screen, then click **Confirm & Save to Memory**. If importing multiple PDFs at once, the app will conveniently step you through each roster confirmation in sequence!
3. **Practice & Navigate:**
   - Click **Study** to start a flashcard session.
   - Click **Class Grid** to view all student photos at once with names hidden.
   - Click **View Class Grid** at any point during a study session to flip over to the roster grid, then click **Resume Study** to jump right back into flashcards!
4. **Light & Dark Mode:** Toggle between Dark Mode and Light Mode anytime using the **☀️ Light / 🌙 Dark** button at the top right of the header bar.
5. **Save Progress:** When you finish studying, click **💾 Save Deck File(s)** to download `.deck.json` progress files to your computer. Next time you open the app, just drag your `.deck.json` files back in to resume right where you left off!

## 💡 How the Flashcards Work (Leitner 5-Box System)

The tool uses a smart 5-box memory system so you spend time on the names you find trickiest:

- **New or missed names** stay in **Box 1** and appear frequently.
- **Correct answers** move a student up to higher boxes (**Boxes 2–5**), so you see them less often as you memorize them.
- **Mistakes** gently return a student to **Box 1** for review.
- **Flexible Practice Modes:** Test yourself with interactive flashcards (guessing the name from the photo), or use the **Class Grid** view with names hidden to practice recalling student faces at your own pace!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><b>1. Can I drop in multiple class roster files at once?</b></summary>
<br>
<b>Yes!</b> You can drag and drop multiple PowerSchool <code>.pdf</code> files and saved <code>.deck.json</code> files into the drop box simultaneously. The app will immediately load all <code>.deck.json</code> files and queue up confirmation modals for each PDF roster.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2. Is any student information sent over the internet?</b></summary>
<br>
<b>No, zero.</b> Everything runs 100% locally inside your web browser. The tool works completely offline—you can even turn off Wi-Fi while using it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3. Is it safe to share `learn-my-students.html` with other teachers?</b></summary>
<br>
<b>Yes!</b> `learn-my-students.html` is just an empty program. When you share `learn-my-students.html` and `README.md` with colleagues, you are only sending them the blank application. None of your student photos or names are inside it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4. How do I save and resume my flashcard progress?</b></summary>
<br>
At the end of a study session, click <b>💾 Save Deck File(s)</b>. This downloads a small <code>.deck.json</code> file to your Downloads folder. To resume studying tomorrow, drag that <code>.deck.json</code> file into `learn-my-students.html`.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5. What format of PDF does PowerSchool need to export?</b></summary>
<br>
Use the standard PowerSchool <b>Photos: 12/page</b> export option. Student ID numbers are automatically stripped out during import for privacy.
</details>
